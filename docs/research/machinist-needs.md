# Research: what machinists need from a drawing-to-CAD tool

Compiled October 21–22, 2026 for the Version 2 build plan. Three research passes: (1) practitioner sources (eMastercam, Practical Machinist, CNCZone, Zeiss quality forum, Elsmar, Eng-Tips, shop and vendor blogs); (2) the competitive landscape; (3) quality, compliance and drafting standards.

**Reading notes.** Some forums block automated reading; evidence taken from search-engine summaries of a thread is marked **(SS)**. Vendor capability and accuracy figures are the vendors' own claims. Legal points are our reading, not legal advice. Several threads are old (2004–2013); the complaints recur in 2024–2026 sources.

| 14 | **Scanned and legacy prints** with explicit uncertainty | Auto-vectorised scans are messy ([PM 336502](https://www.practicalmachinist.com/forum/threads/best-practices-when-digitizing-old-engineering-drawings.336502/), SS); mixed fractions and decimals cause errors ([PM 196296](https://www.practicalmachinist.com/forum/threads/reverse-engineering.196296/), SS) | Medium | 2 (clean scans), later |
| 15 | **Editable parametric output** | Machinists trust output they can see and edit ([CloudNC](https://www.cloudnc.com/blog/cam-assist-for-3-2-axis-components-earning-machinists-trust)); checking AI CAD can take longer than it saves ([Chalmers thesis](https://odr.chalmers.se/items/c654d500-a3dc-40b3-9f29-87a4a4d47c60), SS) | Medium | 6–7 |
| 16 | **Shop-floor packet** (paper ballooned print, hole chart, QR link) | Operators still want paper; oil ruins tablets ([PM 329010](https://www.practicalmachinist.com/forum/threads/digital-drawings-in-the-shop.329010/), SS) | Low-medium | 4 |

**Time cost.** One turnkey shop quoted 40–80 h of CAD for drawing-only jobs ([eMastercam 69496](https://www.emastercam.com/forums/topic/69496-read-pdf-file-into-mcam/)); shops report 5–98% of jobs arriving with models and model fees around $150 ([Zeiss forum](https://qualityforum.zeiss.com/topic/5500-how-do-you-program/)); castings and multi-sheet prints take 60–100+ h ([PM 151242](https://www.practicalmachinist.com/forum/threads/complex-casting-or-involved-blueprint.151242/), SS).

**What machinists do not want:** nominal-only models; a model that disagrees with the print; software that invents datums, tolerances or thread specs; a forced process (fixed tap-drill size); cluttered DXFs; silent unit conversion; black-box "AI"; tablet-only shop floors; expensive seats.

## 2. Competitive landscape (October 2026)

| Product | What it does | Gap for us |
|---|---|---|
| Werk24 ([docs](https://werk24.io/llms.txt), [pricing](https://de.werk24.io/pricing)) | Drawing → JSON (dimensions, GD&T, threads, title block) with locations; Studio does AS9102/PPAP ballooning | No 3D output; EU cloud by default; no handwritten drawings |
| CADDi Drawer ([platform](https://caddi.com/en-us/platform)) | Drawing OCR, similarity search, compare; ITAR tier announced June 2026 | No 3D models; large-manufacturer focus |
| Paperless Parts Wingman 2.0 ([Wingman](https://www.paperlessparts.com/paperless-parts-wingman/), [API](https://paperlessparts.com/api)) | Reads specs, threads, CUI markings for quoting; GovCloud | No 2D→3D; good integration target |
| Backflip Drawing to CAD ([Develop3D, Sept 2026](https://develop3d.com/cad/backflip-converse-2d-engineering-drawings-directly-into-cad/)) | Drawing → parametric Fusion model, STEP | Cloud; no documented check against the print; no ITAR/on-prem |
| Aibuild 2D-to-3D ([use case](https://ai-build.com/use-cases/2d-to-3d/)) | Drives your CAD to build models; on-prem | Enterprise pilots; tolerances need review |
| breptile (open source) ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/breptile/)) | Drawing IR + JSON recipe → deterministic OCCT STEP | Closest architecture to ours; extraction not built |
| CloudNC CAM Assist, Toolpath ([CloudNC](https://cloudnc.com/newsroom), [Toolpath](https://toolpath.com/)) | Toolpaths from an existing model | Need a 3D model first: our output is their input |
| Ideagen QC, High QA, DISCUS, 1factory, Net-Inspect, GroundControl ([GroundControl](https://gndctl.com/industries/machining)) | Ballooning, AS9102 forms, CMM import | Do not make solids |
| ProShop, JobBOSS², E2, Fulcrum, Global Shop | Document control and job records | Integration targets (CSV/watch folder first) |

Instant-quote services (Xometry, Protolabs) still require a 3D model for automation ([Protolabs](https://protolabs.helpdocs.io/l/en/help-center/uploading-cad-files)); a fast checked STEP unlocks those tools. Text-to-CAD tools failed on non-trivial parts in a June 2026 test ([Wevolver](https://www.wevolver.com/article/we-tested-7-text-to-cad-tools-are-they-actually-useful-for-engineers)).

**Opportunities we adopted:** callout-by-callout verification; real self-hosting with a local path that reacts to markings; CAM-ready options (mid-tolerance, hole table, hole colours); question list at quote time; one extraction feeding STEP, DXF, balloons and characteristic lists; datum-aligned STEP and QIF for offline CMM programming; revision delta; flat pricing.

## 3. Quality, compliance and standards

**AS9102 Rev C (2023)** ([Form 3](https://iaqg.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/9102_form_3.doc), [Rev B vs C](https://www.net-inspect.com/blog/as9102-rev-b-vs-rev-c/)). Form 1: part and drawing identity, change notices, full/partial FAI with reason, verified-by and approved-by (different people). Form 2: material and special processes as drawn. Form 3: characteristic number, reference location (sheet/zone), designator, requirement, results, tooling, NC number. Customer rules: one balloon per characteristic, notes ballooned, basic dimensions not, nX features recorded per instance or min/max, results to the drawing's decimals ([example instructions](https://psemc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/AS9102-FAI-instructions.pdf)). New FAI triggers include design, source, process, location or NC-program changes and a two-year lapse ([Lockheed Q2A](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/content/dam/lockheed-martin/aero/documents/scm/Quality-Requirements/Clauses/q2a_rev11.pdf)).
*Data to capture per characteristic:* number (with sub-index), sheet, zone, bounding box, raw text, type, nominal, limits, units, modifiers, tolerance source, designator, instance count, extraction method and model version, confidence, reviewer, result slots, gauge, NC number.

**AS9100 / ISO 9001 records** ([mandatory records list](https://advisera.com/9100academy/knowledgebase/list-of-mandatory-documents-in-as9100-rev-d/)): hashes of uploaded drawings, revision lock, append-only audit (user, time, old/new, reason), separate verify/approve signatures, export bundle on request. ISO 9001:2026 published September 2026 ([DNV](https://www.dnv.com/news/2026/new-edition-of-the-quality-management-system-standard-iso-9001-released/)); IA9100 expected ([Amtivo](https://amtivo.com/us/resources/technical/aerospace-standard-revision/)). FDA QMSR effective February 2, 2026 ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/quality-management-system-regulation-qmsr)).

**Export control (not legal advice).** Drawings are technical data ([22 CFR 120.33](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/22/120.33)); release to a foreign person in the US is an export ([120.50](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/22/120.50), [120.56](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/22/120.56)). The encryption carve-outs ([120.54](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/22/120.54), [15 CFR 734.18](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/15/734.18)) cover end-to-end-encrypted transmission and storage, not processing that decrypts. DFARS 252.204-7012 requires NIST SP 800-171 and FedRAMP Moderate-equivalent cloud services ([DFARS](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/252.204-7012-safeguarding-covered-defense-information-and-cyber-incident-reporting)). AI endpoints at that level exist in AWS GovCloud and Azure Government ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-in-amazon-bedrock-fedramp-high), [Microsoft](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/azuregov/azure-openai-fedramp-high-for-government)).
*CMMC:* contract clauses took effect November 10, 2025. One research pass found Phase 2 (third-party Level 2) scheduled for November 10, 2026 ([Squire Patton Boggs](https://www.squirepattonboggs.com/en/insights/publications/2025/09/the-cmmc-dfars-final-rule-goes-live-ready-or-not-here-it-comes)); another found a July 13, 2026 suspension of Phase 2 pending reform ([Crowell](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/department-of-war-immediately-suspends-cmmc-phase-ii-requirements-launches-60-day-reform-review), [Inside Government Contracts](https://www.insidegovernmentcontracts.com/2026/09/cmmc-reform-task-force-updates-september-2026/)). Verify before relying on either.
*Markings to detect* ([DTIC marking guide](https://discover.dtic.mil/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/GuideToMarkingDocuments_2023.pdf), [CUI dissemination controls](https://www.archives.gov/cui/registry/limited-dissemination), [distribution statements](https://assist.dla.mil/Online/help/dist_stmt.cfm)): CUI banners and designation blocks; NOFORN, FED ONLY, FEDCON, NOCON, DL ONLY, REL TO, DISPLAY ONLY; Distribution Statements B–F and their phrases; the export-control warning (Arms Export Control Act, Export Administration Act, DoD Directive 5230.25); ITAR/USML, 22 CFR 120–130, EAR, 15 CFR 730–774, ECCN, EAR99; FOUO; destruction notices; classification blocks (reject). An unmarked drawing is not necessarily uncontrolled.

**Drafting standards.** ASME Y14.5-2018 (record the edition; concentricity and symmetry removed) ([ASME](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/y14-5-dimensioning-tolerancing)); Rule #1 vs ISO 8015 independency ([Keyence](https://keyence.com/ss/products/measure-sys/gd-and-t/basic/international.jsp)); basic and reference dimensions; title-block tolerances are company-specific and applied by decimal places on inch drawings ([Eng-Tips](https://www.eng-tips.com/threads/default-tolerances-in-title-block.85074/post-316465)); ISO 2768-1 classes f/m/c/v (class m: ±0.1 mm at 0.5–6 mm up to ±2 mm at 2000–4000 mm; ISO 2768-2 withdrawn in favour of ISO 22081) ([Wevolver](https://www.wevolver.com/article/iso-2768-general-tolerances-tables-classes-current-status)); ISO 286 fits (Ø40 H7 = 40.000/40.025) ([Roymech](https://www.roymech.co.uk/Useful_Tables/ISO_Tolerances/how-to-use-iso-tolerances/)); threads per ASME B1.1-2024, ISO 965, ASME B1.20.1 ([ANSI blog](https://blog.ansi.org/ansi/asme-b1-1-2024-unified-inch-screw-threads/)); surface texture per ASME Y14.36 (bare number on inch drawings = Ra µin) ([ASME](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/y14-36-surface-texture-symbols)); projection symbol and US third-angle default; title blocks per ASME Y14.100.

**MBD.** Open CASCADE can write AP242 semantic PMI for dimensions, tolerances and datums attached to shapes ([OCCT XDE guide](https://occt3d.com/dev/doc/overview/html/occt_user_guides__xde.html)); validate with NIST's STEP File Analyzer ([NIST](https://www.nist.gov/services-resources/software/step-file-analyzer-and-viewer)). SolidWorks imports AP242 PMI; Mastercam and Fusion support is weak today, so PMI pays off for inspection more than CAM. QIF 3.0 (ISO 23952) imports into PC-DMIS ([Hexagon](https://docs.hexagonmi.com/pcdmis/2023.1/en/helpcenter/mergedprojects/core/05_file_adv_topics/Importing_a_QIF_File.htm)).
